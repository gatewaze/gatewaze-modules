PyTorch, Powering the Enterprise with Red Hat, NVIDIA, and IBM
210A (Concourse Level)
Co-Located Events
9:00 AM – 3:30 PM

While serving AI models for research and pilot projects is a well-solved challenge, transitioning to 24/7, enterprise-ready systems is the next critical phase of AI maturity. The requirements for reliability, observability, scaling, and concurrency at this level are non-trivial. In response, PyTorch—along with its sister foundation projects and the ecosystem—has begun delivering essential enterprise-level features and enhancements.

Along with these technical advancements, there is a growing understanding of the business use cases, constraints, and growth areas for enterprise AI. Here too, moving AI beyond simple chatbots and images in slide decks requires exploration and hard work—from both the tech industry and the companies striving to deliver real value to their business processes.

Red Hat, NVIDIA, and IBM are hosting a day focused on the evolution of the PyTorch Foundation and its ecosystem, specifically tailored to enterprise use cases. The morning session will feature discussions on business applications and real-world examples of integrating PyTorch into enterprise environments. The afternoon hands-on workshop offers a guided experience, taking participants from business problems and data to a fine-tuned model, served at scale and exposed to users via an agentic application. Come join us for an exciting day of enlightening discussion and real world applications.

LF AI & Data Summit 2026
210B (Concourse Level)
Co-Located Events
9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Join the LF AI & Data community at PyTorch Conference North America for a focused Mini Summit exploring how open source collaboration is shaping the next generation of AI.

The event will bring together developers, maintainers, researchers, practitioners, and technology leaders to share practical experience and explore the open technologies powering AI across the full lifecycle—from data and models to deployment, orchestration, observability, governance, and agentic systems.

Sessions will highlight technical innovation and real-world lessons across topics including agentic and multi-agent AI, MLOps and AI infrastructure, data and document intelligence, model inference and optimization, responsible AI, interoperability, and production deployments.

As AI systems become more complex, open source communities play a critical role in reducing fragmentation, improving interoperability, and giving developers greater choice and flexibility. The Summit will showcase projects and approaches helping organizations build scalable, trustworthy AI systems through open collaboration.

Whether you contribute to an open source project, build with PyTorch, operate AI infrastructure, or are moving AI applications into production, join us to learn from peers, discover technologies across the LF AI & Data ecosystem, and help shape a more open and interoperable future for AI.

Jupyter Day
210C (Concourse Level)
Co-Located Events
9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Jupyter Day, co-located with PyTorch Conference North America, brings together the people shaping the future of interactive computing from data scientists and developers to researchers, educators, open source contributors, and tool builders. As AI transforms how we write code, explore data, conduct research, and build applications, Jupyter is becoming an increasingly important environment for bringing these workflows together. This year’s event will explore how Jupyter is enabling new workflows across data science, scientific computing, visualization, education, AI, and beyond while continuing to advance open, reproducible, and accessible computing.

Through technical talks, hands-on sessions, community discussions, and real-world use cases, Jupyter Day will showcase both what’s possible today and what’s emerging next. From evolving notebook and developer experiences to reproducible research, interactive visualization, open standards, AI-assisted workflows, and new tools across the ecosystem. Attendees will hear from Project Jupyter contributors and the broader community while exchanging ideas about the future of interactive computing.

How to Register: Pre-registration is required. Register for Jupyter Day as a stand alone event or add it to your PyTorch Conference North America registration. Registration Cost: $99

PyTorch Associate Training
210D (Concourse Level)
Co-Located Events
9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

The PyTorch Associate Training is an in-person, instructor-led program designed to equip participants with the foundational and practical skills identified in the domains and competencies of the PyTorch Certified Associate exam.

Whether you aim to launch your deep learning journey, strengthen your current ML/DL capabilities, or gain an industry-recognized credential, this training offers a structured yet hands-on learning experience. Through a blend of lectures, demonstrations, guided labs, and assessments, students will not only understand PyTorch’s ecosystem but will also build and optimize real-world models from scratch.

Upon completion of the course, you will also receive a voucher (value of $250) to take the new PyTorch Certified Associate (PTCA) certification exam.

Pricing: $595 for PyTorch Conference attendees. Includes a voucher for the PyTorch Certified Associate (PTCA) certification exam (value of $250).

How to Register: Pre-registration is required. To register for PyTorch Associate Training, add it to your PyTorch Conference registration through Tuesday, October 13th.
